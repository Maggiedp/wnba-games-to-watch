How this is scored

Overall is a weighted blend: 60% matchup quality plus 40% playoff importance.

Quality is the harmonic mean of the two teams' ESPN BPI ratings, normalized on the live ±8 BPI spread — it rewards games where both teams are strong, not just one.

Importance is the swing in playoff odds this game produces in a Monte Carlo simulation, measured against a season-start ceiling.

Win probability is separate from quality: it's an Elo rating (with a +50 home-court bump), not BPI.
